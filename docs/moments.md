# Moments

Moments are single-image stories that appear in the Moments bar on the home page.

## Posting a moment

1. Click **Add moment** in the Moments bar.
2. Choose one image. Supported formats are PNG, JPEG, JPG, WebP and GIF.
3. Optionally add a caption. Moment captions can be **up to 280 characters**.
4. Click **Post moment**.

## Viewing moments

- The Moments bar shows recent moments, grouped by person.
- Open a group to view its moments one after another. When one person's moments finish, the viewer moves on to the next person.
- Click **Archive** to see your own past moments, and **Back to moments** to return.

## Managing moments

- Delete your own moments from the viewer.
- Moderators can hide other people's moments.
