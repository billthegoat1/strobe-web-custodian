# Posts

Posts are the main way to share photos on Strobe.

## Creating a post

1. Type a caption in the **What's in your mind?** box.
2. Click **Photo or Video** and choose your images. You can attach **up to 10 images** per post.
3. Supported formats are PNG, JPEG, JPG, WebP and GIF.
4. Click **Share**.

A post must have at least one image.

### What happens when you click Share

1. The post is created with your caption.
2. Each image is uploaded one at a time using an upload URL from the API.
3. The post is updated with the uploaded image URLs.

If any upload fails, the half-created post is deleted so you don't end up with an empty post.

## The feed

The home feed loads 10 posts at a time. More posts load as you scroll.

## Interacting with posts

- **Like** or unlike a post with the heart icon.
- **Comment** from the post view. Empty comments are not accepted.
- **Edit** your own caption from the post view. Captions can be up to 500 characters.
- **Delete** your own posts from the post menu.
- Moderators can **hide** other people's posts.

## Profiles

A profile page shows the user's 50 most recent posts, plus their followers and following lists.
